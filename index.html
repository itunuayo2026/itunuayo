Our Story

How It All Began

How I Met My Wife-to-Be ❤️

Funny how the girl who "respectfully ghosted" me for weeks is the same one I'm about to call my wife. If you had told me that back then, I would have laughed you out of the lecture hall 😂

So here's how it happened. We were both doing our MBA at Ajayi Crowther University, Oyo, and it was exam season—everyone buried in textbooks like their lives depended on it, because honestly, they kind of did. I strolled into the hall that day with zero intention of reading. My only mission was to find a friend and probably distract them from their own revision 😂

Then I saw her. Sitting near the front, deep in her books, looking like the definition of "serious student." I told myself I'd just go say hi to my friend… but before I could, she stood up to use the convenience, and our paths crossed right there. That was it. Friend? Forgotten. New mission: find out who this woman was.

I walked up to her with the smoothest line I could find on such short notice: "Your face looks familiar." 😂 In hindsight, not my best work, but it got her attention. We exchanged numbers before she could escape back to her books, and I walked off feeling like an absolute mastermind.

Then came the reality check.

I called—nothing. I texted—nothing. I was basically shouting into a void. If "unresponsive" was a person, it would have been her back then 😂 At some point I just accepted that I'd been respectfully ghosted and moved on with my life.

Fast forward to the very last day of our exams. The whole class was buzzing with relief, everyone celebrating surviving the semester, and out of nowhere, there she was again. This time, no lectures, no pressure—just pure exam-is-over energy. We talked, laughed off the stressful weeks behind us, and took a few pictures together to mark the end of it all.

Sadly, that day didn't fix everything right away—the late replies and slow texts continued for a while. Then, out of nowhere, she wished me a happy birthday, and that simple message changed everything. I later found out she'd had me filed away as "one of those boys" the whole time—little did she know I meant business.

My patience paid off, because I ended up with a real one: caring, loving, accountable, loyal, committed—simply everything I had prayed for. Itunu mi, my love, ololufe mi, my best friend—thank you for finally picking up. I love you, now and forever.

How I Met My Husband-to-Be ❤️

It still amazes me how someone you once saw as a complete stranger can become the most important person in your life. I mean, I ignored this man! I barely picked up his calls throughout our MBA programme. And now, he is becoming my husband. I honestly never saw it coming.

So, back to the beginning. We met during our MBA programme at Ajayi Crowther University, Oyo. I took a short break from reading to go to the convenience, and this tall man approached me with the classic, "Your face looks familiar." 😂 In my head, I was like, "Brother, please. You definitely do not know me from anywhere." 😂

We exchanged numbers, and I already knew I was not going to take the guy seriously. Minutes later, the message, "Hey, Deji is here," came in, and then the calls started coming back-to-back, disturbing my serious-student life 😂

MBA came and went, and I honestly paid him little attention. Then, after the programme, I saw his birthday status. I love wishing people happy birthdays, even when we are not particularly close, so I wished him a happy birthday. Somehow, that simple message started a conversation, and gradually, we began talking more.

And then… the story changed.

What drew me to Ayodeji? He makes me feel free to be myself. He is calm, can be funny, open to correction, and genuinely wants to become better. He listens to me, loves the things of God, and loves people. Most importantly, he makes me feel like his baby❤️

Of course, we have some differences; these are just a few. This man can attend five parties in one day, while I would happily choose sleep over stepping outside. He loves food like it is a full-time job😅, while I can go half the day without remembering to eat. Yet somehow, I have fallen completely in love with our differences.

So yes, the girl who ignored his calls is now getting married to the boy who kept calling 😂

My lover, my best friend, my Panda, Boss Baby, Winnie the Pooh (our inside joke), Ayodeji Temi, Ayinla mi…

My husband❤️

I love you, now and forever.

Our Story

How It All Began

The ACU campus in Oyo was baking under the typical second-semester heat, but inside the lecture hall, the atmosphere was dead serious. Everyone was buried in textbooks, frantically preparing for exams like our entire futures depended on it—because as MBA students, they kind of did.

She was sitting near the front, deeply engrossed in her books, looking like the picture of academic focus. I, on the other hand, had zero intention of studying right then. I was just strolling into the hall on a noble mission: to find a friend and probably distract them from their own revision.

Then, fate intervened in the most unromantic way possible: she needed to pee.

She stood up to head out, and that was the exact moment our paths crossed. The moment I saw her, my brain completely wiped my original plan. Forget my friend; my new priority was finding out who this stunning woman was. I somehow managed to channel enough courage to pull off a classic move—stopping her right outside the hall for a "quick chitchat."

I must have said something right—or maybe she was just eager to get back from her bathroom break—because before she walked away, we exchanged numbers. I walked off feeling like a mastermind.

Then came the reality check.

I called. Crickets. I texted. Radio silence. Calling her "unresponsive" back then would be a massive understatement. I was basically dropping messages into a black hole. At that point, I figured I'd been respectfully ghosted and accepted my fate.

Fast forward to the very last day of our final exams. The relief in the air was palpable. Everyone was celebrating surviving the semester, and out of nowhere, there she was again.

This time, I didn't let the chance slip. No heavy lectures, no pressure—just exam-is-over adrenaline. We started talking, laughed off the stressful weeks behind us, and snapped a few pictures together to mark the end of the semester.

Whatever magic was in those photos did the trick. After that day, the radio silence completely evaporated. The texts actually got replies, the calls lasted longer than thirty seconds, and the rest, as they say, is history.

Who knew a quick bathroom break in an Oyo lecture hall would lead to all this?
